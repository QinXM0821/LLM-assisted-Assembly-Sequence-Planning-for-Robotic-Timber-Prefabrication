4. Free-Edge & Left-to-Right compliant sequence design  
Strategy:  
a. Place bottom plate first.  
b. Move left-to-right across the wall.  
   For each bay, place the left stud, then its nogging while the right side of

c. Finish with the top plate.

5. Conflict check  
At every insertion the member to be placed has at least one long side edge and one end edge fully exposed to free space, so a straight slide-in path exists.
